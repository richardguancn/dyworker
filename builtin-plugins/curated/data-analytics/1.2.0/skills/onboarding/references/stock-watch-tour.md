# Stock watch tour

Use for a request to introduce or tour Stock watch, under onboarding's **Explore a prepared example** instructions.

- **Preview:** append `stock/index.html?view=1` to `baseUrl` in the installed [example catalog](../../../assets/example-catalog.json).
- **Follow-up artifact type:** a stock dashboard.

Introduce the illustrative watchlist, price charts, and what-if Long/Short trade scenarios. Invite the user to select a company or adjust a scenario. Explain that Sites lets people build and share interactive websites like this while exploring them alongside chat.

Prices and scenarios are illustrative, not verified current market data. News cards are a dated snapshot, not a live feed. No site inspection, news browsing, or market-data query is needed for this introduction. Finish with the shared tour's “Create one with my data” follow-up.
